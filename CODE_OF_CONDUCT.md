# Code of conduct

We want data-master to be a useful, careful and welcoming research tool.

Participate with respect. Discuss evidence and implementation choices without
attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, impersonate or
publish private information about another participant. Do not use project
spaces for spam or unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Include enough context to investigate. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical.
